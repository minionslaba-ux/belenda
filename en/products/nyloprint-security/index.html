Description

nyloprint® plates are used in direct and indirect letterpress for fine text and guilloches; nyloprint® WA 116 S Digital for solid and spot UV varnish effects. Water-washable nyloprint® poly chablon plates ink up intaglio gravure cylinders: a soft relief gives optimal ink transfer, and a high-contrast colour change after exposure eases quality checks.
